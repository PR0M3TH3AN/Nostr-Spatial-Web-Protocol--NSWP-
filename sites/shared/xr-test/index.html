Headset door test:

This page is for finding out what a headset's browser does when one site sends you to another while you are in VR. Press Enter VR, then pull the trigger to go through the door. Everything that happens is written below and on the wall in VR, and is carried with you from site to site.

The guided test posts its log publicly to Nostr relays as it goes, so it can be read without copying it out of the headset: what is written below, the answers you press, and this browser's name for itself (which says what kind of device it is), under a key made for each page alone. Nobody is signed in and nothing goes to anyone's feed. Everything under this line is the test by hand, which sends nothing anywhere.
