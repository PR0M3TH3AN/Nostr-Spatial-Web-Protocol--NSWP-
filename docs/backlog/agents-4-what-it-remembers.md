## What it is

A visitor who comes back is remembered, from notes sealed to that visitor and
kept only with their say-so. Memories of one visitor are never mixed with
another's because the agent is the same. A visitor can see, correct, export
and forget what is kept. Only what they approved is sent to a model that isn't
on their device.

## Done looks like

Recall works for a returning visitor; another visitor can't see it; a remote
model gets only approved context.
