## Where it stands

Not built. An item today is a description anyone may copy; nothing limits
how many exist.

## What it needs that nobody here can supply alone

Somebody who keeps count. The plan for paid items
(`docs/plans/paying-for-things.md`, 7.3) already gives one honest kind of
scarcity: an item whose use needs a receipt, which only its seller's
selling service issues. "Only a hundred exist" would be that service
refusing the hundred-and-first sale. Nothing can stop the files being
copied; what can be limited is who holds a receipt.
