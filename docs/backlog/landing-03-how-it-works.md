- **Who:** BitPortal's session.

A page of BitPortal's, not the landing, for someone who wants to know what
they are standing in: what a door is and what the Airlock checks before it
lets them through; how signing in once carries from world to world and what
a world is and isn't told; what goes with them (how they look, their
settings, what they carry, what they have earned); how rooms, voice and
tips work. Each part short, each true of the code that day, each saying
where the fuller account is in the protocol.

## Done looks like

Someone who has never used it can say, after reading, why a door asks
before it takes them somewhere new, and what a world can and can't learn
about them. Every claim checked against the protocol document it rests on.

## Must not

Say a visit is private or safe in a way the code doesn't make true. Call
anything seamless that costs a press.
