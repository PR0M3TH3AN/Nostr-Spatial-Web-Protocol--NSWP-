## What it is

An example Three.js world that loads its agents from a list: where each
stands, what it looks like, and which actions it may take there (move, look,
speak, interact). Example kits: a guide, a companion, a shopkeeper. Everything
is cleaned up at a door or a reload.

## Done looks like

A maker adds an agent by editing data, not code; a visitor sees and talks with
it without joining a room.
