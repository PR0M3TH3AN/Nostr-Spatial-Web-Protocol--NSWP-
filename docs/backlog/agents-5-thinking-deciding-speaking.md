## What it is

Four kinds of provider behind fixed shapes: intelligence (a local model, or
OpenRouter when chosen), System 1 for quick typed choices (simple rules for
free, or JEV), voice (CHIMERA, the browser's own, later ElevenLabs), and
hearing (push to talk). Each falls back to something free, and nothing paid is
switched to without the visitor's say-so. Captions always.

## Done looks like

An agent still follows, acts and talks when its voice or its decision provider
is down; no paid call is made silently.
