## What it is

In a room a visitor chose to join, one agent's state is the same for everyone,
with one member in charge and a safe hand-over when they leave. Separately, an
agent's thinking can be hosted so it goes on after browsers close. A visitor
who joined no room still sees their own local agents. Hosting needs a service
somebody runs and pays for, which is the owner's word.

## Done looks like

Two members of a room see one agent do one thing; someone outside the room
still has their own; a forged leader and a split are both refused.
