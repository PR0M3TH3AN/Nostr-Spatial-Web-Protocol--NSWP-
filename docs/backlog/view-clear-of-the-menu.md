# A world should be able to keep its view clear of the client's menu

- **Asked:** 2026-11-24, by the orchestrator, from BitPortal's step 2 (its
  PR 209). Not an ask of the owner's.
- **Who:** the NSWP builder, with step 3 of `docs/plans/the-clients-menu.md`.
- **State:** not built, and no code yet. Written into the plan as a piece of
  step 3 (2026-11-24, section 15, "The view clear of the menu"), with an
  answer proposed to each thing under "To settle" below. **The word's name
  is given: `covers`** (the orchestrator, 2026-11-24), answered through the
  two calls a world's own menu has, whoever draws. The plan says what its
  numbers are measured in and from, and that all four edges are said.

## What is missing

Where its client draws the menu, a world doesn't know what of its view the
menu covers: the bar along the bottom, the marks at the top, and the sheet
while it is up. A world that drew its own menu knew, and kept what matters
clear of it (a caption, a thing being looked at, its own interface). Now it
can't.

## Wanted

- The client says how much of the view its menu covers: the bar, and the
  sheet while it is up.
- A world may shift its view by it, or keep its own things out from under
  it.
- Said only when it changes: nothing crosses while nothing changes, as with
  every other word (`docs/plans/the-clients-menu-cost.md`).

## To settle in the plan

- What is said: edges taken off the view (so much from the bottom, so much
  from the top), or the boxes themselves. Edges are less to say and can't
  describe a sheet that floats in the middle.
- Whether a world is told of the sheet at all, or only of what is always
  there (the bar and the marks): the sheet is up for a moment, and a view
  that shifts each time it comes and goes may be worse than one that
  doesn't.
- The words and their limits, and a vector for each, as for every word.
