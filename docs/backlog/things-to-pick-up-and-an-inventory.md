# Things in a world you can pick up, and an inventory to choose between them

- **Asked:** 2026-11-25, by the owner, in the `nswp-agents` session: "i
  need to work with bitportal orchestrator on a visual way to display
  items in the world that can be picked up and used as inventory."
- **Who:** a design first, with the owner, by the orchestrator; then the
  kit (the NSWP builder) and BitPortal's Bag; the playground's agents
  take the same declaration.
- **State:** asked. The design conversation is the orchestrator's to
  start with him.

## What exists today

- A visitor's Bag (the kit's, and BitPortal's over every world) lists the
  items they own, published as notes, and one can be held in the hand:
  the dart blaster, whose uses are its own ("Fire", "Reload").
- In the agents' playground, a thing in a room can be picked up, carried
  and put down, and an item declares what it can be used for, by name.
- Not yet: a thing lying in a world that a visitor can pick up into what
  they carry; carrying several and choosing between them; and how any of
  that looks in the world and on the screen.

## For the design to settle (the orchestrator's list, for the owner)

- What a thing in the world that can be picked up looks like, so a
  visitor knows before touching it.
- What picking it up does: into the Bag (kept, published, across worlds),
  or into the hands for this world only, or both, the world deciding.
- How several carried things are shown and chosen between: a row of
  slots on the bar, the Bag's tab, a wheel; at a desk, on a phone, in a
  headset.
- What "use" offers for a thing in hand, from the uses it declares.
- The same declaration for a person's things and an agent's (the
  playground's finding: one declaration for a thing that can be carried
  and used, its uses by name).

## Must not

- Let a world take or change what is in a person's Bag without their
  press.
