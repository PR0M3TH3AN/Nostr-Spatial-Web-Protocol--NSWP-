## Where it stands

An achievement in one world is built and live as a claim (NSWP-16, kind
37307; the Arcade has four; BitLogin signs one). Shown on a person's card
among what they have done in a world. An achievement granted by someone a
reader trusts, and ones that span worlds, are proposed.

## What it needs that nobody here can supply alone

For "granted": the world's own key signing that this person earned it,
which needs the world to have somewhere that key can sign (a server, or
its maker by hand). For "across worlds": someone to define them and say
which worlds count, as for shared boards.
