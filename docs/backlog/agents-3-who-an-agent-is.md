## What it is

An agent is a signed note by its owner; its SOUL and other documents are
signed notes beside it; a world's placement of it is signed by that world's
publisher. A SOUL changes personality and never what an agent may do. The
pack's candidate kinds are 37309, 37310 and 37311, the next after those NSWP
uses; they are to be given by the orchestrator (fleet rule 11) and checked
against the wider registry first. Publishing goes through the sign-in address,
once it allows those kinds.
