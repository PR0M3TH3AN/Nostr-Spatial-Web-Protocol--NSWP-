# BitLogin from a headset: rare, and one press out and one press back

- **Asked:** 2026-11-25, by the owner, on learning that no web page (so no
  BitLogin panel) can be shown in immersive VR: "This is a serious issue",
  then "Yes please do that. just make it as rare and a seemless as
  possible. And if there are anythings we can do to enable BitBrowser to
  make things even more seemless lets add that to BitBrowsers TODO list as
  well."
- **Who:** the NSWP builder (the kit's half); BitPortal (its shell and
  room); BitBrowser (what a browser of our own can do better, on its TODO).
- **State:** asked.

## Why

In immersive WebXR a browser shows only what the world draws; no page
element, so no BitLogin panel, wallet, Files, Allow or sign-in. That holds
in every browser. A world drawing BitLogin's screens itself can't be told
from a fake (`sign-in-inside-a-headset`, and the pause of phone pairing).

## Wanted

1. **Rare.** In a headset nothing everyday needs BitLogin: a visitor is a
   guest at once; one sign-in serves the family; notes, reactions, the bag
   and room passes sign with no press. Only the wallet, Files, buying, the
   first sign-in to an account, securing a guest, and an Allow for
   something careful need its screens. A check lists every place the kit
   and BitPortal would ask BitLogin in a headset, and each is either
   removed or marked as one of those.
2. **One press out.** Such a press in a headset ends the immersive session
   and opens BitLogin's real panel at once, full size, in the browser's
   ordinary view (the shell's panel under BitPortal, the kit's client panel
   or window otherwise), with nothing else between. Not "leave the headset
   view for a moment", but done for them.
3. **One press back.** While the panel is up, and when it closes, a large
   "Back into VR" press over the page; one press re-enters immersive VR
   where they were (place, facing, what was in hand), as a door does.
4. Said plainly, once, before the first time: "This opens BitLogin
   outside VR for a moment. Press Back into VR when you're done."

## For BitBrowser (its TODO)

A browser of our own can do what a web page can't: draw BitLogin's screens
in VR as the browser's own, which no world can draw over or fake (a
floating panel with the browser's own address and lock, over the
immersive scene), and so keep a visitor in VR for sign-in, the wallet and
pairing safely. Also: keeping the place on the way out and back, passkeys
in VR.

## Must not

- Draw BitLogin's screens in a world's page, or let a world see what is
  typed there.
