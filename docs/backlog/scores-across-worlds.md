## Where it stands

A board for one world is built and live (NSWP-15, kind 37306; the Arcade
keeps one; BitLogin signs a score). A board across worlds is written in
NSWP-15 as proposed and not built.

## What it needs that nobody here can supply alone

Someone to say which worlds' scores count on a shared board, and what a
score on it means. That is a list somebody keeps and signs: BitPortal's own
curator at first, any curator a visitor chooses later, the way lists of
worlds work (NSWP-08). Without it a shared board is whatever any world
claims.
